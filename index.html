02 / How I work

Four principles I work by

01

A compelling, ROI-driven why.

You need to understand clearly why you're doing something and what return it will bring. Build the business case around ROI, and make sure it stays clear and defensible even when it's scrutinized at the lowest level of detail.

02

Course-correct early and often.

Consistent communication keeps corrections small. Lots of small adjustments along the way are far easier than a major pivot once a program has gone off the rails.

03

Gemba: go and see.

In-person experience is the most valuable kind. When a few buildings in Japan needed safety controller upgrades inside two-hour downtime windows, I went and did them myself.

04

Deliver value in increments.

Find ways to deliver value a piece at a time. Small, consistent wins build momentum, excitement and buy-in.
